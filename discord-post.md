# Auto Rail Bridges

Laying track across a pit is two clicks per tile: bridge, then rail, over and
over. Aim a rail at open water or an unbridged pit and nothing happens at all.

This makes it one click. Place a rail where it cannot go and a bridge from your
inventory goes down underneath it first. Carrying several kinds? It reaches for
the highest-priority one you actually have — Glass, then Stone, then Wood, then
Scarlet, Coral, Galaxite, Gleam Wood, Metal Grate and Industrial Bridge. The
clips below show the same crossing built with wood and with stone.

On solid ground nothing changes; rails behave exactly as they always have.
Carrying no bridge at all is harmless — the rail just drops as a pickup item,
nothing is consumed.

## Good to know

The bridge order is fixed in this version; making it configurable is planned.
The on/off switch in **Options → Mod settings** applies after a restart,
because part of the work happens while the world loads.

## Compatibility

Works with **PlacementPlus**, grid placement included — drag a rectangle of
rails across a pit and each one gets its own bridge, for as long as your
bridges last.

## Requirements

**CoreLib** and **Mod Settings Menu**, both offered when you subscribe. In
multiplayer the server needs it too, because it changes what the world
considers a valid placement.
