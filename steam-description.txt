[h1]Auto Rail Bridges[/h1]

Placing a rail across a pit or over open water normally does nothing at all — the rail has no ground to sit on, so the game refuses the placement. This mod turns that into a single click: place a rail where there's no ground, and a bridge from your inventory goes down underneath it automatically.

When you're carrying more than one bridge type, the mod always reaches for the same one first: Glass, then Stone, then Wood, then Scarlet, Coral, Galaxite, Gleam Wood, Metal Grate, and finally Industrial Bridge. That priority order is fixed in this version.

[b]No bridge, no placement — but nothing is lost.[/b] If you aren't carrying any bridge at all, the rail simply drops back as a pickup item instead of being wasted. Pick it up, bring bridges, and try again. On solid ground, rails behave exactly as they always have — this only changes what happens over a pit or water.

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]

Works alongside PlacementPlus, including its grid placement — drag a rectangle of rails across a pit and each one gets its own bridge, for as long as your bridges last.

[h2]Settings[/h2]

Toggle the mod on or off in-game under Options → Mod settings. The change takes effect after restarting the game, since part of the mod's work happens while the world is being loaded.

[b]Multiplayer:[/b] install this mod on both the client and the server. It changes what the world considers a valid placement, so both sides need to agree.
